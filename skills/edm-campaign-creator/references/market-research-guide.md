# Market Research Guide for EDM Campaigns

## Why Market Research is Critical (Phase 0)

**Real-World Example**: WinBridge EDM Transformation

**Before Research** (Product-Driven):
- "Our 40W amplifier has Bluetooth 5.0"
- Focus: Product features
- Result: Generic messaging

**After Research** (Demand-Driven):
- Discovered: 45% teachers, 20% tour guides, 15% fitness coaches
- Discovered: Primary pain point is vocal strain, not volume
- Result: "Your voice is your career—protect it"

**Lesson**: 30 minutes of research improved the entire campaign strategy.

## Research Checklist

### 1. Industry Analysis

**Questions to Answer:**
- [ ] What industries use this product/service?
- [ ] What is the market size in target countries?
- [ ] What are the seasonal trends?
- [ ] Who are the main competitors?

**Research Methods:**
```
1. Web Search
   - "[product] industry analysis USA"
   - "[product] market size 2026"
   - "[product] trends"

2. Competitor Analysis
   - Subscribe to competitor emails
   - Analyze their messaging
   - Note their offers and timing

3. Customer Review Mining
   - Amazon reviews
   - Industry forums
   - Reddit discussions
```

**Template: Industry Distribution**
```
| Industry | % of Market | Pain Points | Best Timing |
|----------|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| Teachers | 45% | Vocal strain, large classes | Aug-Sep (Back to School) |
| Tour Guides | 20% | Outdoor noise, long days | Apr-May (Pre-season) |
| Fitness Coaches | 15% | Music, large classes | Jan (New Year fitness) |
| Business | 8% | Meetings, presentations | Year-round |
| Other | 12% | Various | Various |
```

### 2. Customer Persona Development

**Create 3-4 Detailed Personas**

**Persona Template:**
```
Name: [Descriptive Name]
Role: [Job title]
Age: [Range]
Location: [Country/City type]

DAILY LIFE:
- Typical day schedule
- Work environment
- Challenges faced

PAIN POINTS:
- Top 3 frustrations
- Consequences of not solving
- What have they tried

MOTIVATIONS:
- Career goals
- Personal values
- What drives purchases

BUYING BEHAVIOR:
- Budget range
- Decision timeline
- Information sources
- Purchase triggers

MESSAGING ANGLES:
- What resonates emotionally
- What proof they need
- What objections they have
```

**Example: Sarah the Teacher**
```
Name: "Struggling Sarah"
Role: High School Science Teacher
Age: 32
Location: Suburban Texas

DAILY LIFE:
- Teaches 5 classes/day, 35 students each
- Lab work requires moving around room
- Parents evenings and extra duties

PAIN POINTS:
1. Loses voice by Wednesday every week
2. Students in back can't hear demonstrations
3. Throat pain affects sleep and mood

MOTIVATIONS:
- Wants to teach for 30+ years
- Cares deeply about student success
- Values health and sustainability

BUYING BEHAVIOR:
- Budget: $50-150 (personal purchase)
- Timeline: Needs solution NOW
- Sources: Colleague recommendations, Amazon reviews
- Triggers: Particularly bad week, seeing colleague use one

MESSAGING ANGLES:
- "Protect your career" resonates
- Needs social proof from other teachers
- Worries about "looking weak" using amplifier
```

### 3. Pain Point Discovery

**The 5-Why Method**

Start with surface problem, dig deeper:

```
Problem: "I need a voice amplifier"

Why? "Students can't hear me in the back"
  ↓
Why? "I'm not loud enough"
  ↓
Why? "I don't want to shout"
  ↓
Why? "My throat hurts when I shout"
  ↓
Why? "I want to teach for 30 years without damaging my voice"

ROOT PAIN POINT: Career longevity and health
```

**Pain Point Priority Matrix**
```
                    HIGH URGENCY
                          │
     Career-threatening  │  Daily frustration
     (vocal damage)      │  (throat pain daily)
                          │
LOW IMPACT ───────────────┼──────────────── HIGH IMPACT
                          │
     Minor inconvenience  │  Occasional issue
     (slight hoarseness)  │  (tired after long day)
                          │
                    LOW URGENCY
```

**Focus on HIGH IMPACT + HIGH URGENCY quadrant**

### 4. Competitor EDM Analysis

**Sign up for competitor emails and track:**

**Tracking Template:**
```
| Brand | Date | Subject Line | Tone | Offer | CTA | Notes |
|-------|------|--------------|------|-------|-----|-------|
| BrandA | 3/1 | "20% OFF" | Aggressive | 20% off | Shop Now | Too pushy |
| BrandB | 3/5 | "Protect your voice" | Caring | Free guide | Learn More | Good angle |
```

**What to Look For:**
- [ ] Subject line patterns
- [ ] Email frequency
- [ ] Offer types (discount % vs. value-add)
- [ ] Tone and voice
- [ ] Design style
- [ ] CTA language
- [ ] Timing and seasonality

**Differentiation Opportunities:**
- What are they NOT saying?
- What pain points are they missing?
- How can we be more authentic?

---

### 5. Solution-Product Mapping

**Map Customer Pain to Your Solution**

```
CUSTOMER PAIN → SOLUTION VISION → YOUR PRODUCT → PROOF

Example 1:
"Throat hurts daily" → "Teach pain-free" → 18hr battery → "I teach 5 days straight"

Example 2:
"Students can't hear" → "Everyone understands" → 40W coverage → "Back row hears clearly"

Example 3:
"Worried about career" → "Teach for 30 years" → Medical-grade → "Used by 100k+ teachers"
```

**Create for each persona:**
- Their #1 pain point
- The solution they desire
- How your product delivers
- Proof (testimonials, data)

## Quick Research Methods

### When Time is Limited (30 minutes)

**15 min: Web Search**
- Search: "[product] for [audience] reviews"
- Read 5-10 Amazon reviews (look for pain points)
- Check 2-3 competitor websites

**10 min: Persona Sketch**
- Create 2 basic personas
- Define their #1 pain point
- Note their likely objections

**5 min: Messaging Notes**
- Write 3 potential subject line angles
- Identify 1 unique value proposition
- Note any seasonal timing insights

## Common Research Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Assuming you know the customer**
   - Always validate with real data

❌ **Focusing only on demographics**
   - Psychographics (pain points, motivations) matter more

❌ **Ignoring competitor research**
   - Learn from their successes and failures

❌ **Skipping the "why"**
   - Surface-level pain points lead to weak messaging

❌ **Researching but not documenting**
   - Write it down or you'll forget key insights
